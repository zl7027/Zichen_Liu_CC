Project Description

This is an interactive creative coding project that combines animation, graphic design, sound, and user input. By clicking/dragging the mouse, or pressing different keys on the keyboard, people can create a variety of sounds and colorful, dynamic garphic shapes on the screen. You can also switch music genres by tapping the bottom-right corner.

Why I Like It

What I found interesting is that, although the interaction is very simple, every part of the screen and every key on the keyboard triggers a different visual and auditory response.

Furthermore, when people drag the mouse, a series of chain reactions occurs. The interactive experience feels like playing a musical instrument. As people interact with it, they experience not only auditory engagement but also a visual impact. This impact is created by stacking simple graphic shapes and patterns.

Another aspect is that the interactive process gives people the feeling that they are creating their own music.

Week 8 Teamwork Assignment

The Worlds

Throughout this project, I learned a lot about how to combine individual creative ideas into one interactive experience. We started by designing our own worlds, and I created a glowing mushroom forest using OOP, randomization, and simple animations. At first, each of our sketches worked independently, but combining them into one program was more challenging than I expected. We had to modify our code to work with graphics buffers and make sure all the animations could run together. Setting up the projector was also a new experience for me, especially when we had to adjust the positions and mapping to fit the physical surfaces. But I think that experience was very fun, to see the sketches play on the boxes. One of our biggest challenges was the worm GIF, which stopped animating after playing once. We spent a lot of time debugging and testing different approaches. This process made me realize that even when individual parts work perfectly, bringing them together can create unexpected problems. Overall, I really enjoyed seeing our different visual styles come together, and this project helped me better understand both the technical and collaborative sides of creating an interactive installation.
